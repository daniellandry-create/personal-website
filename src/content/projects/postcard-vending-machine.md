## Overview

Built with Design For America at the Yale Schwarzman Center, this
project gives Yale student artists a physical outlet to showcase and
sell postcard prints of their work on campus -- a mini gallery and
retail machine in one, designed and built entirely from scratch.

## What I built

- **Team leadership.** Leading 10+ students through the design and
  build of the machine end to end, from concept through iterative
  testing.
- **Mechanical & electrical design.** Designing the machine's
  mechanical and electrical systems from scratch -- CAD modeling,
  electronics selection and wiring, and rapid-prototyping the
  dispensing mechanism -- on a $1,000 budget.
- **Program management.** Overseeing materials procurement and
  iterative testing, balancing scope against the budget and timeline.
